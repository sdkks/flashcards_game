Published collection: 155 English trivia decks, 4,264 questions. OpenTDB and LearnClash content and factual review date: 16 October 2026. Friends, How I Met Your Mother, The Walking Dead and Star Trek: The Original Series metadata retrieval and review date: 17 October 2026. The collection includes introductory science, nature, geography, history, arts, transport, television, film, sports, food and traditional mythology. It is not a balanced world-regions syllabus or comprehensive world history. Some decks are narrow introductions and are not medical, legal, safety or conservation advice. Modern-franchise plot and relationship spoilers are not included in these introductory decks, and narrative decks are not published or cleared by these terms; linked sources may contain material beyond this scope. Interface language does not translate the cards.

The playable CSVs are the public deck content. Question wording, selection and arrangement are offered under the applicable terms below. This notice states the attribution, license, change-notice and scope conditions that apply to the collection; it does not license application code, source prose, images, media, trademarks or other third-party material.

The 4,264 English questions comprise 53 LearnClash adaptations, 14 OpenTDB adaptations, 33 TVmaze-derived records and 4,164 original contributor questions (421 previously summarized plus 3,743 newly integrated contributor-authored cards). These categories reconcile to the published total: 53 + 14 + 33 + 4,164 = 4,264.

Collection at a glance

  • 155 English trivia decks with 4,264 questions, plus seven separate language and AI study decks.
  • Science & Nature includes introductory astronomy, chemistry, biology, anatomy, ecology, botany, invertebrates, optics, geology and paleontology.
  • History and geography includes world history, ancient civilizations, medieval castles, human origins, aviation, cities, water landscapes and a short Aotearoa New Zealand documentary-history round.
  • Arts, culture and literature includes Japanese woodblock-print materials and techniques, literature, traditional mythology and Greek underworld traditions.
  • Transport and technology includes vehicle engineering, railways, bicycles and motorcycles.
  • Television and film includes original-series cast and production questions for Friends, How I Met Your Mother, The Walking Dead and Star Trek: The Original Series, plus original-production questions about Star Wars: A New Hope.
  • Sports and food provide compact introductory rounds rather than comprehensive surveys.
