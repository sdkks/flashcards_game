# Trivia catalog

This page contains the public English trivia inventory and its category/Lucky behavior. Deck content remains English when the interface is localized. The [starter trivia notice](../public/sample_games/starter-trivia-notices.html) contains the public source, license, attribution and scope terms.

### English starter trivia

One hundred fifty-five bundled English trivia decks contain **4,264 questions** (162 published decks including the seven legacy study decks). The public rights accounting is 53 LearnClash adaptations + 14 OpenTDB adaptations + 33 TVmaze-derived records + 4,164 original contributor questions (421 previously summarized and 3,743 newly integrated contributor-authored cards) = 4,264. ### Categories and I'm feeling lucky

The catalog has one hundred fifty-five English trivia decks (4,264 questions) and seven legacy study decks, for 162 published decks. The browser separates three actions: arrows expand categories, category names set the Lucky scope, and deck buttons load a deck. A parent category includes its descendants. Search matches deck names and category paths but does not change the displayed Lucky scope; clearing search restores the ordinary expanded branches. The current deck stays visible even when filtered out of the list.

**I'm feeling lucky** chooses one eligible English trivia deck, then leaves the Flip/Type choice to you without starting a timer. An unfiltered draw uses the general-trivia decks. Selecting **Trivia** includes all 155 trivia decks; selecting a subject category includes its descendants. Searching or expanding categories alone does not change the Lucky scope. Direct selection is available independently of Lucky. The seven legacy language/AI decks remain directly playable but are not eligible for Lucky. No round mixes questions from different decks. Random draws may repeat; choosing the current untouched built-in deck does not reset a running session. An empty eligible scope explains the problem instead of silently choosing elsewhere.
